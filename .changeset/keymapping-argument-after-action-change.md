The keymapping editor saves the argument you type after you change the action. Before, the mapping kept its old argument, such as a number after a change from Scroll lines to Send text, and ignored what you typed until the settings were loaded again. The scroll amount field no longer puts the old number back while you type, so you can empty it or type a minus sign first. (by [@janah01](https://github.com/janah01))
