"obsidian-terminal": patch

Clear terminal, Close terminal and Find in terminal now show in the command palette when you open it from the ribbon while the terminal you used last is the active tab. They act on that terminal. Their hotkeys now also work in a terminal you type in after another terminal opened behind it. From a note, their hotkeys still do what they did before, except that they no longer clear, close or search a terminal in another window. Toggle focus on last terminal and Focus on last terminal now go from a note to the terminal you used last when it is in another window. Before, they stayed in the note. (by [@janah01](https://github.com/janah01))
